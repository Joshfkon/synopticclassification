# SCS Amendment: Anchored Spanning Works

## Summary of Change

Spanning works (V suffix) are now **anchored to their center of gravity era** rather than floating to the base regional number. The V suffix indicates the work extends beyond that era, keeping it shelved near related material.

### Compound Suffixes

When a book is both a spanning work AND has a thematic focus, use compound suffixes with V first:

| Suffix | Content | Example |
|--------|---------|---------|
| VS | Spanning + social commentary | Wilkerson's *Warmth of Other Suns* |
| VE | Spanning + economic analysis | Gordon's *Rise and Fall of American Growth* |
| VF | Spanning + foreign policy | Isaacson's *The Wise Men* |
| VP | Spanning + political commentary | |

The V indicates "this work extends beyond the indicated era" while the second letter indicates the thematic lens. Plain V without a second letter is for neutral spanning works with no dominant thematic lens.

### Anchoring Spanning Works

**Anchor spanning works to their center of gravity**—the era that dominates the book's focus or argument—rather than floating them to the base regional number. This keeps spanning works shelved near chronologically related material.

**To determine center of gravity:**
1. Where does the book spend most of its pages?
2. What era is central to the book's argument or thesis?
3. Where would a reader most expect to find it?

**Examples:**
- Gordon's *Rise and Fall of American Growth* → **973.91VE GOR** (centered on industrial-era transformation, extends to present)
- Wilkerson's *Warmth of Other Suns* → **973.91VS WIL** (centered on Great Migration, extends into late 20th century)
- Isaacson's *The Wise Men* → **973.918VF ISA** (centered on Truman-era policy formation, extends through Cold War)
- Weiner's *Legacy of Ashes* → **973.921VF WEI** (centered on early CIA/Cold War, extends to present)

**Reserve base-number V (e.g., 973V) only for:**
- True survey works covering all eras without a dominant center
- Textbook-style comprehensive histories
- Reference works that resist chronological anchoring

### How It Works

**No suffix** = narrative history (what happened)
**Letter suffix** = thematic material (analysis, commentary, policy debates)
**V suffix** = extends beyond the indicated era

Examples:
- 973.924 → Narrative history of Nixon era
- 973.924P → Political commentary about Nixon era
- 973.924S → Social policy debates during Nixon era
- 973.918VF → Spanning foreign policy work centered on Truman era
- 973R → Reference/textbook on US government (timeless)
- 973V → True all-eras survey of the US (no center of gravity)

### Spanning Works

Books covering multiple eras are **anchored to their center of gravity** with a V suffix indicating extension beyond that period.

**Determining center of gravity:**
- Where does the book spend most of its pages?
- What era is central to the argument?
- Where would a reader expect to find it shelved?

**Examples:**
- *The Wise Men* (Isaacson) → 973.918VF (Truman-era center, Cold War span)
- *Rise and Fall of American Growth* (Gordon) → 973.91VE (industrial era center)
- *Warmth of Other Suns* (Wilkerson) → 973.91VS (Great Migration center)

**True all-eras surveys** with no dominant center use the base regional number:
- A complete history of Russia → 947V or 947.01
- A comprehensive US history textbook → 973V
